Using this site

Privacy and data

What this site collects, what happens to contributions, and your rights

The short version

This website does not set cookies, does not use tracking pixels and does not run analytics. Nothing you do while reading is recorded by us or by anyone working for us.

The one thing the site stores is your choice of light or dark theme. It is kept in your browser’s local storage on your own device, it is never sent to us, and it is not a cookie. Clearing your browser data removes it.

Website hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub’s servers record standard technical information (IP address, user agent, requested page) in server logs for security and reliability, as described in GitHub’s privacy statement. We do not access those logs for research or analytics.

Map tiles

The interactive map requests map images from OpenStreetMap’s public tile servers. That request tells OpenStreetMap which part of the map you looked at and your IP address; see the OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy. If you would prefer not to make that request, use the list of entries on the map page instead — it is the same information, loaded from this site.

Contribution forms and emails

The get involved form is hosted by our own form endpoint on Cloudflare Workers; responses arrive in a university-managed account. Emails you send us are stored in that same account. We ask for contact details so we can acknowledge contributions and confirm consent, and for the information needed to describe what you have contributed.

Lawful basis. Research contribution data is processed as a public task (research) under UK GDPR, with consent recorded for publication.

Retention. Contribution records, consent forms and correspondence are kept for the life of the project and then for the period required by the university’s retention schedule. Material deposited in an archive is governed by the deposit agreement you sign with that archive.

Sharing. We do not sell data or share it with commercial third parties. Contributions may be passed to a named partner archive only with your written agreement, and map data is published under an open licence once you have consented to publication.

Your rights. You can ask for a copy of what we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected, anonymised or erased, and withdraw consent for future use. Email hello@example.ac.uk; we will reply within one month.

Reusing this site’s content

Text on this site is available under a Creative Commons licence unless stated otherwise; the map dataset is released under CC0. Images and archive material may have different terms — ask before reuse. The underlying site files are open source so the site can be rebuilt or rehosted later.

Video Communities Revisited

Video Communities Revisited is a research project on the role of physical screen media — VHS, Betamax, DVD, rental shops, collectors, swap meets and boutique labels — in forming communities in Britain, past and present.
