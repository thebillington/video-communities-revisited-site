Ways to take part

Tell us about a shop, club or swap meet. Add it to the public map, or send us the details and we will add it. Name, town, rough dates and whatever you remember is enough to start — a proper address is a bonus, and we will always credit what you tell us.

Send a memory. A paragraph, a photograph, or an hour of conversation. We are collecting memories of rental shops, staff, regulars, tape trading, film clubs and the end of the rental era.

Lend or offer objects. Tapes, membership cards, staff handbooks, shop signage, catalogues, equipment, newsletters. We can arrange photography, scanning or digitisation, and return everything.

Point us at an archive. Local newspaper runs, trade directories, business records, a video collection in a library store, or a group already doing this work.

Volunteer. We work with volunteers on cataloguing, transcription and checking map entries against directories.

Add a place to the map

The quickest route is the form below. If you would rather not fill in a form, email hello@example.ac.uk with the name of the shop or club, the town, roughly when it traded, and anything you remember.

Please do not send us anything you do not have the right to share. Photographs of people need their permission; if in doubt, tell us about the image instead and we will work out the rights together.

What happens to your contribution

  • We acknowledge receipt, usually within a fortnight.
  • A person reads it and decides with you how it should be used.
  • Map entries appear on this site with a credit line you approve.
  • Memories may become a story on the site, part of a publication or a deposit in a partner archive — we ask again before that happens.
  • You can ask us to correct, anonymise or remove your contribution at any time.

Consent and ethics

This project has ethical approval from [University ethics committee reference]. Contributions are handled under the project’s data management plan. In short:

  • Consent is recorded before anything is published, and re-confirmed before material is reused in research outputs.
  • Anonymity is available on request, for you and for anyone you mention.
  • Copyright stays with you; you give us permission to publish the contribution on this site and in project outputs, and nothing more.
  • Withdrawal is possible at any point before publication, and after publication where material can reasonably be removed.

Our full approach is set out in the privacy and data note. If anything here is unclear, contact us before you send anything.

Send a contribution

The online form is being built. For now, contributions arrive best by email — it reaches the same place, and we read every message.

hello@example.ac.uk

Please include: your name (or "anonymous"), the name of the shop, club or collection, the town, roughly when it was active, and your memory of it. Photographs are welcome, but only if you have the right to share them.

Video Communities Revisited

Video Communities Revisited is a research project on the role of physical screen media — VHS, Betamax, DVD, rental shops, collectors, swap meets and boutique labels — in forming communities in Britain, past and present.
